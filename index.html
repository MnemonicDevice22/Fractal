How to use

Fractal holds the chores that never finish. You add one once, and it stays.

Each chore moves through three states. Inactive is resting. Due means it should happen soon. In progress means you're doing it right now, and a clock runs while you are.

Stop the clock and the time is saved next to the chore, then it goes back to Inactive. Six squares, ten minutes a square, two rows for the hour. Over an hour it just says the number.

Once you know how long something takes, lock it and the clock stops running for good. You can also type the time in yourself.

Up next shows the first three that are Due. Finish one and the fourth takes its place.

Extras are for the rare things that aren't chores. Tick them, then clear them.

Nothing here counts days or keeps score. It waits.
